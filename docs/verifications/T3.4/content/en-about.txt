ABOUT
LOCAL KNOWLEDGE, REAL TRAILS

EnduroFun is a small team of riders and guides based in Álora, running multi-day enduro routes across the wildest parts of the province of Málaga.

OUR STORY
RIDERS FIRST, GUIDES BY TRADE

What started as a handful of friends exploring the trails around Álora on weekends grew into a small guided-tour operation, built on years of riding the same terrain in every season and condition. Every route we run has been ridden by us first — we know where the mud sits after rain, which climbs need a run-up, and where to stop for the best views.
